How Tipwise works

Your meal is unique. Tipwise uses a few details to create a quick estimate, so you can make the final call with confidence.

01

Share the meal details

Enter the bill amount, party size, meal time, day, and the two requested table details.

02

Get a model estimate

Tipwise encodes those inputs and sends them to the trained XGBoost model for a prediction.

03

Choose what feels right

Use the suggested amount as a reference. Your experience and your judgment always come first.

A helpful guide, not a rule. The estimate reflects patterns learned from the model’s training data; it may not capture every detail of your service or local tipping customs.
